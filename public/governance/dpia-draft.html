STEP 1

Identify the need for a DPIA

Primovex is a practice management system used for stock/medicines inventory, cold-chain temperature monitoring, compliance recording, and governance case management — specifically Patient Safety Concerns (following the NHS Wales "Listening to People" pathway) and Subject Access Requests. It is a new system, introduced alongside the practice's core national systems (EMIS, Docman), not a replacement for them.

At least one ICO screening trigger applies, which is sufficient on its own to require a DPIA:

  • Special category data. Concerns and SARs both relate to patient care and, even pseudonymised, fall within Article 9 health data by association.
  • New technology / novel processing for this practice. This data flow (governance case management in a dedicated tool) has not previously been assessed.
  • Systematic, practice-wide processing used continuously by multiple staff roles.

Conclusion: a DPIA is required before Primovex's governance modules (Concerns, SARs) are relied upon as the practice's primary record for these processes.

STEP 2

Describe the processing

Nature

Collection: manually entered by practice staff via desktop or mobile forms at the point a concern, SAR, stock movement, or temperature reading occurs.

Use: case tracking against statutory/pathway deadlines, correspondence logging, stock and cold-chain safety monitoring, staff-facing reporting (including annual category/outcome breakdowns for external returns such as the Welsh Risk Pool).

Storage: Google Cloud Firestore, EU multi-region (eur3); application server logic (Cloud Functions) runs in europe-west2 (London, UK).

Deletion: governance timeline, correspondence, and stock-movement entries are immutable audit records (cannot be edited or deleted by design). Stock items are soft-deleted (archived, recoverable). Concern cases and user accounts can be hard-deleted, restricted to System Admin only, behind an explicit confirmation step.

Sharing: Concerns correspondence is logged with named external bodies as part of the normal governance process — see the accompanying data-sharing map for the full list and current protocol status.

Special category data: yes, by association — patient identifiers (EMIS number, or initials + date of birth) and, for the person raising a concern when that differs from the patient, initials and a contact method. No patient name, address, or full date-of-birth-plus-name combination is stored; that identifying detail is deliberately kept in the paper file, cross-referenced only by the Primovex case reference.

Context

Patients do not interact with Primovex directly and have no visibility into it as a distinct system — their expectation is that a concern they raise, or a SAR they submit, is handled by "the practice," not that it necessarily reaches a specific piece of software. Patients raising a Concern shortly after a clinical incident may be a vulnerable cohort at the point data is recorded, which is a reason for the deliberate identifier minimisation already built in, not a reason to avoid recording the case. No prior complaints or concerns about this specific processing have been identified, and the underlying technology (a cloud-hosted practice management tool) is a standard, unexceptional category of system.

Purposes

To record and manage patient safety concerns and SARs within their statutory/pathway timescales; to maintain an auditable correspondence trail; to support medicines cold-chain safety through temperature monitoring; to maintain accurate stock levels, reducing clinical risk from unavailable medicines or equipment. The intended effect on individuals is faster, more consistent handling of their concern or request, and a lower risk of a cold-chain failure affecting medicines administered to them.

STEP 3

Consultation process

To be completed by the practice This draft was produced from technical review, not staff or patient consultation. Before sign-off, consider recording:
  • Consultation with the staff who will use Concerns/SARs day-to-day (reception, secretarial, clinical) on whether the recorded fields and workflow match how they actually handle a case.
  • Consultation with BCUHB's Information Governance team, given the data-sharing implications described in Step 4 and the accompanying data-sharing map.
  • Whether patient/PPG (Patient Participation Group) input is appropriate or practical for a system patients don't interact with directly — a judgement call for the DPO, not something this review can settle.

Individual rightsPatients exercise access/rectification/erasure rights through the practice's existing EMIS/SAR process; Primovex has no direct patient-facing interface through which a right would be exercised against it specifically.
Processor arrangementsGoogle Cloud (Firebase) is a processor under a standard Data Processing Addendum. To be completedConfirm the DPA has been formally reviewed and is on file.
International transfersData resides in the EU (eur3); application logic runs in the UK (europe-west2). No transfer outside the EU/UK identified in the current configuration.
