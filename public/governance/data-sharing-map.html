Data-sharing map · for WASPI / ISP review

Who Primovex data currently flows to

Every external organisation Primovex's Concerns module logs correspondence with, compiled from the categories built into the system. This is not itself a Wales Accord on the Sharing of Personal Information (WASPI) Information Sharing Protocol (ISP) — it's the input list for checking whether an existing BCUHB/health-board ISP already covers each flow, or whether a new one or a Data Disclosure Agreement is needed.

Why this matters now

Primovex has always let staff log that a concern was shared with, say, MDDUS or the police — but until this review, nothing had checked whether that sharing sits under an agreed protocol. That's exactly the gap WASPI exists to close. None of these flows are new because of Primovex; Primovex just makes them visible and auditable for the first time.

External bodies

What's shared is consistently limited to: case reference, category, an anonymised summary, and correspondence dates/notes — never the patient's name, and never more identifying detail than the pseudonymised identifier already described in the DPIA.

Betsi Cadwaladr University Health Board (BCUHB)

Protocol status: to confirm

The practice's health board. Concerns categorised as originating from or escalated to BCUHB, and any case requiring health-board-level oversight.

Data shared: case reference, category, summary, datesDirection: practice → health board

Llais

Protocol status: to confirm

The Welsh patient-voice body (replaced PALS and the former Community Health Councils in 2023). Concerns raised through or referred to Llais.

Data shared: case reference, category, summaryDirection: bidirectional (referral in, updates out)

MDDUS

Protocol status: to confirm

Medical defence / indemnity organisation. Concerns flagged as requiring MDDUS advice (clinical risk, potential claim exposure).

Data shared: case reference, category, summary, clinical-review flagDirection: practice → MDDUS (advice sought)

GMPI

Protocol status: to confirm

Recorded alongside MDDUS as the indemnity/advice reference for concerns requiring external clinical-risk input. (Primovex stores this as a reference field tied to the same "MDDUS required" flag — confirm the exact organisation/scheme this refers to for your practice if it differs from the above.)

Data shared: case reference, GMPI reference numberDirection: practice → GMPI

Solicitors

Protocol status: to confirm

Either the complainant's own legal representation, or the practice's, depending on the case. Recorded as a distinct "Source" category and as a solicitor reference field on both Concerns and SARs.

Data shared: case/SAR reference, category, summary, correspondence logDirection: bidirectional

Coroner

Protocol status: to confirm

Concerns connected to a coronial process — inherently the most sensitive category in the list, typically following a death.

Data shared: case reference, category, summary, correspondence logDirection: practice → coroner (information provided on request)

Police

Protocol status: to confirm

Concerns involving a police referral or investigation (e.g. North Wales Police, depending on the practice's force area).

Data shared: case reference, category, summaryDirection: practice → police (information provided on request)

Welsh Risk Pool

Protocol status: to confirm

Not a per-case correspondence flow, but a distinct annual reporting obligation: Primovex tracks a "Learning from Events" report status (not required / in progress / sent) per concern, feeding the practice's Welsh Risk Pool submission.

Data shared: aggregate category/outcome counts, individual LFE report status per caseDirection: practice → Welsh Risk Pool

Ombudsman

No current data (defined, not yet used)

Public Services Ombudsman for Wales. Available as a Source category in Primovex but no case has been recorded against it in the data reviewed.

Recommended next step

For each body above marked "to confirm," ask your IG/WASPI contact (likely via BCUHB) whether an existing Information Sharing Protocol already covers "GP practice governance correspondence with [body]," or whether this specific flow needs its own ISP or Data Disclosure Agreement. Several of these (BCUHB itself, the health-board-mediated bodies) are likely already covered by standing NHS Wales arrangements; the ones most likely to need a fresh look are the less routine ones — coroner, police, and solicitor correspondence — since those tend to be handled case-by-case rather than under a standing agreement.
