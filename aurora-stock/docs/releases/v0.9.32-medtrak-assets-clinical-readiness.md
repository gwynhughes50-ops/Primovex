# v0.9.32 - MedTrak Assets: Clinical Readiness

## Summary

Sprint 32 introduces the first MedTrak Assets pattern by redesigning Emergency Drugs & Equipment and Anaphylaxis Boxes as clinical readiness assets rather than plain stock tables.

## Added

- Shared `ClinicalAssetChecklist` component for emergency kits and anaphylaxis boxes.
- MedTrak Asset IDs for physical kits, for example `EDK-*` and `ANX-*`.
- QR label generation and print/save workflow for physical kit labels.
- Clinical Readiness score based on missing, expired, expiring and overdue verification items.
- MedAI readiness notes for kit-level operational prompts.
- Print-friendly asset checklist export with readiness summary.
- Asset label service for future QR/NFC workflows.

## Improved

- Emergency Drugs tab now presents a readiness dashboard rather than a spreadsheet-style checklist.
- Anaphylaxis Boxes tab now explains why kit QR codes are used when individual vial barcodes are unavailable.
- Inputs, cards, text and controls now use theme-aware Tailwind tokens such as `bg-card`, `text-foreground`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-border` and `bg-background`.
- Mobile/tablet friendliness improved through responsive grids and larger touch targets.

## Notes

- QR codes currently use an online QR image service for label generation. A local QR renderer can be added later if offline label creation becomes a requirement.
- Existing Firestore collections are preserved: `emergency_assets` and `anaphylaxis_boxes`.
- Existing monthly check saving remains compatible, with additional readiness metadata stored in new check records.
