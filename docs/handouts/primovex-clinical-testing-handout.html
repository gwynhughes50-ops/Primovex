Primovex on your phone

Testing guide for HCAs and nurses  ·  Thursday meeting

Thank you for helping to test Primovex. It is the practice's own system for stock, fridge checks, kits and boxes, and everyday reporting. You do the job on your phone, and Primovex keeps the record: who did it, what, and when. You are testing the system, not being tested. If something is confusing, slow or wrong, that is exactly what we need to hear.

Two golden rules
  • Never type a patient's name into Primovex: not in a note, a message, an issue or the Orb. Use the EMIS number or initials only.
  • Do the real job properly. Fridge readings, kit checks and stock counts you save are real records. If you want to practise an out-of-range fridge reading, tell the Practice Manager first, because it sends an alert.

1. Signing in

  1. Install Primovex from the invite email sent to you, and open it.
  2. The first time, sign in with your email address and the password you set from your invite link.
  3. Choose a 6-digit PIN (and fingerprint or face if you want). After that you only need the PIN or your fingerprint.
  4. Primovex locks itself after 15 minutes without use. Enter your PIN to carry on; whatever you were doing is still there.

Forgotten your PIN? Choose "sign out instead" and sign in again with your password.

2. Your home screen

The big blue button scans stock. The tiles below it are your everyday jobs. The round Orb in the middle of the bottom bar is the assistant: tap it and type or speak a question (for example "how many saline are in the treatment room?"). The bottom bar also has Home, Stock, Checks and Me.

3. The jobs, step by step

Using stock

  1. Tap Scan stock and point the camera at the barcode (or tap Stock and search by name).
  2. Choose how many you used, and confirm. Stock goes down by that number and Primovex records who used it.

Fridge check (twice a day)

  1. Tap Fridge check and scan the tag on the fridge (or tap the tag with the phone). You cannot pick the fridge from a list: scanning shows you were there.
  2. Answer one question at a time: what it reads now, the minimum (press MIN on the thermometer), the maximum (press MAX), then press reset on the thermometer.
  3. The last screen says in range or out of range. Tap Save check. Your name and the time are added for you.
  4. If it is out of range, tick what you did about it. Primovex opens an incident and alerts the people who deal with them.

If the fridge has already been checked this morning or afternoon, Primovex tells you who did it.

Kits and boxes (emergency and anaphylaxis)

  1. Tap Kits and boxes and choose the kit.
  2. Go through the items one at a time. If an item is fine, confirm it. If it is Missing or Expired, Primovex asks what you will do: replace it from stock, borrow from another box, request an urgent order, or follow up later (you can also message a colleague).
  3. Review and save. This is the record that the kit was checked.

Stock take: counting a room

  1. Tap Stock take. If a manager has asked for one you will see it. Otherwise choose Start my own and pick the rooms you will count.
  2. Tap a room to claim it, so nobody else counts the same room. You do not see what the system expects: count what is really there.
  3. Scan each product (or search by name) and enter how many there are. Scan the next one.
  4. Something not in the system? Tap Add an item that isn't in the system. Pick the name from the NHS medicine list as you type, or type it yourself (gloves, syringes). Add the strength, form, brand, barcode, category, how many, expiry and batch if you can.
  5. If a scan says the product is recorded in another room, and this room keeps its own supply, tap Add it to this room.
  6. When the room is done, tap Finish this place. The manager then reviews the differences. No stock figure changes until they apply it.

Report an issue, a significant event, a quick note, messages

  • Report an issue (a fault, something missing, a room problem): say what and where. It goes to the right person.
  • Significant event: something that happened or nearly happened. Describe it factually using roles ("the nurse"), not names. Add an EMIS number only if needed.
  • Quick note is a private reminder for yourself on your own phone.
  • Messages from colleagues appear in the middle of the screen. Reply with one tap (Done, On it, Will do, Thanks, Can't today) or type a short reply, or snooze it.

Tell us straight away if
  • a button does nothing, a screen goes blank, or the app closes;
  • something you saved does not appear, or a number looks wrong;
  • you cannot find where to do something you expected to do;
  • anything shows a patient's name, or someone can see something they should not.

5. Nurse manager extras

  • Request a stock take (on the desktop, Inventory > Stock take): choose a name, who is asked, a finish-by date, and the rooms. Every room set up in Practice Admin is offered, even one with nothing recorded yet, so a first count can start from nothing.
  • Review and apply: counts wait for you. Differences are listed biggest first. Tick the ones you accept, then apply them to stock. Nothing changes before that.
  • Items found that aren't in the system arrive with everything the counter entered. Press Create all as stock items to make them in one go, each in the room it was counted with the number counted as its stock. Any that are already in stock, or were found twice, are held back for you to decide.
  • Fridge incidents: out-of-range checks open an incident. You can take action on it (quarantine, record the stock, clear it).
  • Your home shows what needs attention (such as fridge incidents, expired and low stock) and the Orb gives you a briefing. The Orb's colour tells you at a glance: green all fine, amber something coming up, red something failed or overdue.
