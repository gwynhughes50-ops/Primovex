Timesheets and mileage

A guide for everyone at the practice  ·  on your phone or on a computer

Timesheets and mileage claims are now done in Primovex, instead of the spreadsheets and paper forms. The idea is that it is quick: most months you do one tap, and your line manager only needs to look closely at the ones where something changed.

Every step is recorded (who, when and any reason) and nobody can change or remove the record afterwards. That protects you as well as the practice.

1. Sending your timesheet Everyone

Do this at the end of each pay period. On your phone: open Primovex, go to the Me tab and find Timesheet. On a computer: open Operations, then Timesheet.

You will see the pay period, the date it is due, and your usual hours for the period (worked out from your working pattern, with bank holidays included).

My hours were as normal

  1. Press No changes.
  2. Read the sentence and tick I confirm this is correct.
  3. Press Send to my line manager.

That is all. It takes a few seconds.

Something was different

  1. Press Something was different.
  2. Add each day: choose the day, what it was, the hours, and a comment (needed for “Other”). Press Add this.
  3. Tick the confirmation and press Send to my line manager.

Not ready? Press Save, send later.

What to choose for “what it was”

ChooseWhen
Overtime, standard rateExtra hours you worked, at the normal rate
Overtime, time and a halfExtra hours you worked that are paid at time and a half
Time off in lieuHours of TOIL you took in this period
Annual leaveHours of leave you took
Sick leaveHours you were off sick (the hours only: no reason is needed)
OtherAnything else, with a comment saying what
Leave, sickness and TOIL are still booked in BrightHR as before. Put them on your timesheet as well, so payroll has them.

If it is sent back to you

If your line manager declines it, or payroll returns it, you get an alert on your phone with the reason, and the reason shows on your timesheet. Correct it and press Send to my line manager again. Sent it by mistake? Tell your line manager before they sign it off: they can decline it so you can fix it.

Reminders

If you have not sent yours, Primovex reminds you 5 days before it is due, 2 days before, on the day, and then every day it is overdue. Make sure alerts are on (Me tab, Phone alerts) so they reach your lock screen.

2. Line managers: signing off Line managers

You only see timesheets that name you as the line manager. Open Operations, Timesheet, To sign off (on a phone: Me tab, Timesheet, To sign off). You get an alert each morning while some are waiting.

People whose hours were as normal

They are in one list with the time each person confirmed.

  1. Check the names look right.
  2. Press Sign off all.

Need to query one? Press Decline… next to their name instead.

People who recorded changes

Each is shown in full: their usual hours and every change, with the totals.

  1. Read the changes.
  2. Press Sign off, or Decline….
  3. If declining, type the reason. They see it exactly as you write it.

You cannot sign off your own. If your own line manager is not set, whoever looks after timesheets signs yours off. Under any timesheet, Show the history lists every step with names, times and reasons.

3. Mileage claims Everyone Line managers approve

For work journeys in your own vehicle. Open Operations, Mileage on a computer, or the Me tab, Mileage on your phone.

  1. Press Add a journey.
  2. Choose the date, then From and To from the lists. The miles fill in for routes we know; otherwise type the miles one way.
  3. Tick There and back for a return trip. Choose your vehicle and the reason.
  4. Press Send for approval. You can change or withdraw it until it has been decided.
Never type a patient's name or address. That is why places are chosen from a list. For a patient visit choose Home visit; nothing more is needed.

Line managers approve in To approve (or the Me tab on the phone). Approved miles are paid through payroll at the HMRC rate: 45p a mile for a car or van up to 10,000 miles in the tax year (6 April to 5 April), then 25p; motorcycle 24p; bicycle 20p. Primovex works out the amount for you.

4. The payroll team Payroll team

Open Operations, Timesheet, Payroll and choose the pay period. You only see timesheets a line manager has signed off, with each person's payroll number and approved mileage for the period beside them.

  1. Press Download CSV to put the figures into the payroll system.
  2. Tick the timesheets you have sent (or Select all), add a reference if you want one, and press Mark as sent to payroll.
  3. Something wrong (for example a missing payroll number)? Press Return… and type the reason. It goes back to the person to correct, and then through their line manager again.

You get a daily alert while signed-off timesheets are waiting to be sent.

5. Setting it up Practice Manager

Do this once. It needs a computer.

  1. Bring in the staff. Operations, Timesheets (plural), tab Import from BrightHR. Choose the BrightHR Holiday Summary Report (CSV) and press Import. Leave “doctors and trainees” unticked.
  2. Set each person's working pattern. On People and patterns press Set pattern. Set the days and times and the unpaid break. Use Split shift for two parts to a day, Repeats every 2 weeks for a rota, Zero hours for staff with no usual hours, and New pattern from a date when hours change.
  3. Choose their line manager and link their Primovex account from the dropdowns on the same row. A person cannot see their timesheet until they are linked, and a manager cannot sign off until their own account is linked too.
  4. Add the pay period. Operations, Timesheet (singular), tab Pay periods, Add the next pay period. Enter the name, the first and last day, and the day timesheets are due, then save. Reminders count from the due date.
  5. Mileage places and routes. Operations, Mileage, Places and routes. Add other places staff drive to and the miles between places people often travel between.
  6. Give out the permissions (Admin, Roles): the payroll team role needs Timesheets: the payroll team; whoever helps set up needs Timesheets: set up everyone's working pattern.
Each pay period afterwards: add the next pay period, then use the Pay periods tab to see how many are with managers, signed off and sent to payroll, and exactly who has not sent theirs yet.

6. Common questions

It says my sign-in isn't linked to a staff record.The Practice Manager needs to link your account (Timesheets, People).
It shows no usual hours.Your working pattern has not been set up yet. Ask the Practice Manager.
Nothing waiting for me to sign off.Either nobody has sent theirs yet, or the staff record doesn't name you as their line manager (and your account linked).
I need to correct something after sending.Ask your line manager to decline it. It comes back to you to fix and send again. Once it is signed off it cannot be changed.
My hours changed for good.Ask the Practice Manager to add a new working pattern from the date it changed.
A journey isn't in the list of places.Type the miles yourself and tell the Practice Manager, who can add the place or route for next time.
I'm stuck.Tap the Orb and ask in your own words (for example “how do I send my timesheet?”). It gives the steps.

Questions about timesheets or mileage: ask the Practice Manager.
