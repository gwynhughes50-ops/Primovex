# Sprint 35.1 — Practice Manager Gold Workflow

## Purpose
Create a mobile-first executive overview for Practice Managers without replacing the existing Welsh Listening to People concerns workflow.

## Principles
- Management attention, not a generic task list.
- Routine concerns remain with operational leads.
- Medium/high-risk concerns surface for management review or executive oversight.
- Operational issues are routed to a suggested team and retain an audit history.
- Practice Managers may intervene, but are not assumed to perform every operational action.

## Added
- Practice Manager executive mobile home.
- Operational health by domain.
- Concerns intelligence split into operational, management review and executive oversight.
- Smart operational issue routing with notes, suggested ownership and optional due date.
- Local-first escalation audit scaffold pending an approved Firestore rules sprint.

## Protected
The core Welsh concerns process, deadlines, calculations, Firestore rules, authentication, inventory logic, Pulse calculations, desktop layout, Tauri and branding remain unchanged.
