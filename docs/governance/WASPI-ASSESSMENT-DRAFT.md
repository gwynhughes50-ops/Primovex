## Agreement selection

Choose the applicable WASPI arrangement before any live exchange:

- Information Sharing Protocol for regular reciprocal controller-to-controller sharing.
- Data Disclosure Agreement for one-way controller disclosure.
- Joint Controller Agreement where purposes and means are jointly determined.
- Data Processing Agreement where Primovex processes data on documented controller instructions.

## Required schedules

- Data items and minimisation justification.
- Source, recipient, frequency and transfer mechanism.
- Roles, access levels and authentication controls.
- Retention, review, deletion and legal-hold process.
- Data-quality correction and individual-rights handling.
- Incident reporting and escalation contacts.
- Sub-processors, locations and international transfers.
- Termination, export and verified deletion.

No WASPI agreement is considered complete until the controller has completed the DPIA, selected the correct relationship, populated the official template and obtained authorised signatures.
