# v0.9.30 - Platform Modes & Demo Centre 2.0

## Summary
Sprint 30 introduces platform-level environment switching so MedTrak+ can be used safely for live operations, product demonstrations, staff training and staging/testing.

## Added
- Platform modes: Live, Demo, Training and Staging.
- Persistent mode storage using local browser state.
- Global platform mode banner across the authenticated desktop app.
- Admin controls under Practice Administration > Platform Mode.
- Login-screen platform mode controls for fast demonstration setup.
- Demo Centre upgraded to reflect the selected mode and scenario.
- One-click reset for demo/training data.
- Scenario selector for GP Practice, Research Practice, Large Health Centre and Training Mode.

## Safety principles
- Live mode is clearly labelled as the production system.
- Demo, Training and Staging display visible warnings and use synthetic data.
- Users are reminded never to enter live patient-identifiable information into synthetic environments.
- Switching modes is deliberate and visible.
