# Pulse Nexus

Pulse Nexus is the live operational health signal for MedTrak+.

## Design target

Pulse should communicate practice health in under one second.

The compact orb uses:

- a liquid Nexus ring
- the score as the primary visual element
- a subtle heartbeat/aura
- severity-aware event badge
- hover preview
- double-click to open
- drag-anywhere desktop behaviour

## Interaction model

- Hover: quick preview
- Double-click: open or close drawer
- Click and hold/drag: move the orb
- Right-click: Pulse settings
- Escape/click outside: close drawer
- Mobile: bottom sheet rather than draggable desktop orb

## Principle

The orb stays simple. Detail belongs in the drawer.
