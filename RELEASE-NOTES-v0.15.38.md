# Primovex v0.15.38 — Governed Audit Ledger Foundation

Primovex now has a central, server-authoritative governance ledger and a working Audit Centre.

## Highlights

- Authenticated identity can no longer be supplied or impersonated by the browser audit payload.
- Audit records are append-only and chained per practice.
- Sensitive metadata is minimised before storage.
- Security Centre can search and export governed evidence.
- ClinFlow view, route, triage, claim, priority, completion, archive, summary/original download, print and verification-opening activity is covered.
- Orb records governed intent and disclosure evidence without storing microphone audio or raw questions.
- Inventory, equipment, connected-device assignment and Practice Administration changes forward central evidence.
- Governance operating responsibilities and limitations are documented.

## Important

Real patient data remains disabled. Controller, DPO, Caldicott Guardian, Records Manager and Clinical Safety Officer approvals are still required before any live clinical pilot.
